Ready for your English assessment?

Prepare your own device with Safe Exam Browser (SEB) for supervised English assessments in Toledo. First check your setup at home; then use the official assessment environment in class.

Assessment checklist
  • Install Safe Exam Browser before test day.
  • Use the practice environment first to test Toledo, audio and submission.
  • On the day itself, open the official exam environment and enter the Toledo access code provided in class.

02 · During your supervised assessment

Official assessment

Open this configuration only when your lecturer tells you to. Your device will remain in the secure assessment environment until the lecturer authorises you to leave.

There is no extra password to launch SEB. Your lecturer gives you the Toledo assessment access code in class.

Windows / macOS · download iPad · open in SEB

Do not use the official configuration for practice. Its exit password is held by the invigilator.

Install SEB first: Windows & macOS download · iPadOS app. On an iPad, open this page in Safari and then tap “iPad · open in SEB”.

How it works

STEP 01

Install & practise

Install SEB, open the practice configuration, sign in to Toledo and complete the practice assessment assigned by your lecturer.

STEP 02

Prepare for test day

Bring a charged device, charger and headphones if needed. Connect and check audio before the assessment; close other applications.

STEP 03

Take & submit

Open the official configuration in class, enter the Toledo access code, complete the test and select Verzenden. Wait for submission confirmation.

Can't open Toledo or play the video?

Report problems before the assessment. During a supervised test, raise your hand and wait for the lecturer; do not try to switch apps or restart the session yourself.

What if I use an iPad or an unsupported device?

Test the full workflow in advance. iPad users should open this webpage in Safari, not inside SEB. If your device is not compatible, contact your lecturer early for an alternative.
