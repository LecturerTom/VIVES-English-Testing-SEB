What to do on test day

  1. Bring a charged device, your charger and headphones. Connect and test your headphones before the test.
  2. Open the SEB configuration using the instructions for your device.
  3. Sign in to Toledo within Safe Exam Browser.
  4. Wait for your lecturer to give you the Blackboard test access code in the classroom.
  5. At the end of the test, click Verzenden and confirm your submission before leaving.
Important: This page provides only the SEB configuration, not the test or its access code. Do not try to exit SEB or open other apps during the assessment. For technical problems, raise your hand and wait for the lecturer.
