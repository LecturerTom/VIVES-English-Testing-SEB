Before your assessment

Follow the specific instructions provided in your Toledo course.

01 / Prepare in advance

  1. Install SEB on your own device.
  2. Open the configuration using the instructions above.
  3. Complete any practice test provided by your lecturer.
  4. Check that Toledo works and, if your test includes listening, that your headphones and audio work.

02 / On test day

  1. Bring a charged device and your headphones, if required.
  2. Close other applications and start SEB using this configuration.
  3. Sign in to Toledo and find the assessment for your English course.
  4. Enter the Toledo access code provided by your lecturer.
  5. At the end, select Verzenden and check the submission confirmation.

Do I need an SEB password?

No separate password is required to open this SEB configuration. Your lecturer may give you an access code for the Toledo assessment in class.

Something isn't working?

Test your setup before the assessment. If you have problems with your device, login or audio, contact your lecturer. During the test, raise your hand rather than trying to leave SEB.

Important: This website only provides the software setup, not the assessment or its access code. Follow your lecturer's instructions and stay in the authorised exam environment until your submission has been confirmed.
