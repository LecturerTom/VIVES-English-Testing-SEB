Ready for your English assessment?

Prepare your own device with Safe Exam Browser (SEB) for supervised English assessments in Toledo. First check your setup at home; then use the official assessment environment in class.

Assessment checklist
  • Install Safe Exam Browser before test day.
  • Use the practice environment first to test Toledo, audio and submission.
  • On the day itself, open the official exam environment and enter the Toledo access code provided in class.
!

Important — at home, use Practice SEB only

Never launch the Official Assessment configuration at home. It may lock your laptop or iPad in the assessment environment, and you may not be able to close SEB without help from your lecturer. For all preparation, select Practice & device check. Downloading the file alone does not start SEB; opening it does. If you have opened Official SEB by mistake, contact your lecturer immediately.

01 · Before test day

Practice & device check

Use this configuration at home to check that SEB, the Toledo login, questions, video, headphones and submission work correctly.

When you reach the final page of the Toledo practice test, copy the exit password. Select Submit (Verzenden) and wait for confirmation. Only then close SEB using the password.

Practice exit password: practice2026. If pasting is unavailable on your device, type it manually.

Windows / macOS · download iPad · open in SEB

The practice configuration opens Toledo; your lecturer must provide a separate practice test in Toledo. The practice password is not a Toledo access code.

02 · During your supervised assessment

Official assessment

Open this configuration only when your lecturer tells you to. Your device will remain in the secure assessment environment until the lecturer authorises you to leave.

There is no extra password to launch SEB. Your lecturer gives you the Toledo assessment access code in class.

The SEB exit password appears on the final page of your Toledo assessment. Copy it before submitting your answers. After submission is confirmed, select Quit / Exit and paste the password (or type it manually).

Do not open this file at home. Launching the official configuration may leave you unable to exit without help from your lecturer.

How it works

STEP 01

Install & practise

Install SEB, open the practice configuration, sign in to Toledo and complete the practice assessment assigned by your lecturer.

STEP 02

Prepare for test day

Bring a charged device, charger and headphones if needed. Connect and check audio before launching Official SEB; close other applications.

STEP 03

Submit & exit

On the final Toledo page, copy the SEB exit password. Select Verzenden and wait for confirmation. Only then select Quit / Exit in SEB and paste the password or type it manually.

Can't open Toledo or play the video?

Report problems before the assessment. During a supervised test, raise your hand and wait for the lecturer; do not try to switch apps or restart the session yourself.

What if I use an iPad or an unsupported device?

Test the full workflow in advance. iPad users should open this webpage in Safari, not inside SEB. If your device is not compatible, contact your lecturer early for an alternative.
